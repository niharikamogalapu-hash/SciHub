## NEW GAMES 74-80: Implementation Details

### Game 74: Development & Growth (Memory) ✨ NEW
- **Type**: Memory Pairs Game
- **Lesson Focus**: Piaget stages, attachment theory, parenting styles
- **Pair Count**: 12 matched pairs
- **Content Highlights**:
  - Piaget's four cognitive development stages
  - Attachment security and insecurity outcomes
  - Authoritative vs authoritarian vs permissive parenting
  - All pairs link developmental concepts with explanations

### Game 75: Adolescent Development (Drag & Drop) ✨ NEW
- **Type**: Drag and Drop
- **Lesson Focus**: Physical, cognitive, and social-emotional changes in adolescence
- **Categories**: 3 major domains
- **Content Details**:
  - **Physical Development**: Puberty, secondary sexual characteristics, brain development
  - **Cognitive Development**: Formal operations, risk assessment, metacognition
  - **Social-Emotional Development**: Identity formation, peer relationships, autonomy

### Game 76: Adult Development & Aging (Matching) ✨ NEW
- **Type**: Matching Game
- **Lesson Focus**: Erikson's adult stages, physical and cognitive aging
- **Pair Count**: 6 major concepts
- **Content Coverage**:
  - Early/Middle/Late Adulthood stages and characteristics
  - Physical aging (cellular, organ, sensory changes)
  - Cognitive aging (fluid vs crystallized intelligence)
  - Social-emotional development across adult lifespan

### Game 77: Life Stages & Well-being (Puzzle) ✨ NEW
- **Type**: Puzzle Game
- **Lesson Focus**: Complete lifespan (Erikson's 8 stages + integration)
- **Piece Count**: 10 puzzle pieces in sequence
- **Content Structure**:
  - 8 life stages from infancy to late adulthood
  - Erikson's psychosocial crises for each stage
  - Success/failure outcomes and resolution
  - Cumulative nature of development

### Game 78: Psychological Disorders (Reaction) ✨ NEW
- **Type**: Reaction Speed Game
- **Lesson Focus**: Anxiety, OCD, PTSD, mood, bipolar disorders
- **Item Count**: 10 key terms
- **Content Includes**:
  - Disorder names and primary characteristics
  - Brain structures involved (amygdala)
  - Neurotransmitter systems
  - Rapid recognition practice

### Game 79: Treatment & Therapy Approaches (Builder) ✨ NEW
- **Type**: Builder Game
- **Lesson Focus**: Psychotherapy types and biomedical treatments
- **Element Count**: 10 detailed treatment approaches
- **Therapy Types Covered**:
  - Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  - Psychodynamic Therapy
  - Humanistic Therapy
  - Biomedical: Antidepressants, Antipsychotics, Anxiolytics
  - ECT, TMS, Eclectic Integration
  - Prevention & Resilience

### Game 80: Social Psychology & Applications (Quiz) ✨ NEW
- **Type**: Quiz Game
- **Lesson Focus**: Social thinking, conformity, obedience, prejudice, group behavior
- **Question Count**: 5 comprehensive questions
- **Major Topics**:
  - Attribution theory and biases
